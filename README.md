# Futures Research & Data Accuracy Audit Portfolio

A three-project, primary-source market research and data-verification portfolio focused on U.S. futures prop firms, macro release analysis, and directory data hygiene.

## Projects Included

### 1. Six-Firm Futures Prop Comparison (30+ Decision Criteria)
- **Deliverables:** [`project-1-firm-comparison.html`](project-1-firm-comparison.html) · [`Project-1-Firm-Comparison-Report.pdf`](Project-1-Firm-Comparison-Report.pdf) · [`Project-1-Comparison-Spreadsheet.xlsx`](Project-1-Comparison-Spreadsheet.xlsx) · [`Project-1-Comparison-Spreadsheet.csv`](Project-1-Comparison-Spreadsheet.csv)
- **Scope:** Side-by-side comparison of six major U.S. futures prop firms (**Topstep, Apex, Take Profit Trader, MyFundedFutures, Tradeify, Bulenox**) across 30+ evaluation, drawdown, payout, fee, and rule criteria, backed by date-checked primary help-center URLs.

### 2. Macro Market Brief — NFP Miss & Gold (XAU/USD / COMEX GC)
- **Deliverables:** [`project-2-market-brief.html`](project-2-market-brief.html) · [`Project-2-Futures-Market-Brief.pdf`](Project-2-Futures-Market-Brief.pdf)
- **Scope:** Concise one-page trader brief analyzing the June 2026 Nonfarm Payrolls miss and its reaction in gold, verified directly against the official Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) release (`+57K` jobs, `4.2%` unemployment, `-74K` prior revisions).

### 3. 50-Point Data Accuracy Audit (13 Verified Discrepancies)
- **Deliverables:** [`project-3-data-audit.html`](project-3-data-audit.html) · [`Project-3-Data-Accuracy-Audit.pdf`](Project-3-Data-Accuracy-Audit.pdf) · [`Project-3-Audit-Spreadsheet.xlsx`](Project-3-Audit-Spreadsheet.xlsx) · [`Project-3-Audit-Spreadsheet.csv`](Project-3-Audit-Spreadsheet.csv)
- **Scope:** Systematic 50-point audit comparing public directory listings against official firm help centers. Identified **13 sourced discrepancies** (including outdated FAQ rules, missing account SKUs, promo wording gaps, and coverage omissions) with structured remediation recommendations.
